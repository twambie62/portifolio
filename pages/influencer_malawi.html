Case Study

Project Overview

Influencer Malawi is a digital marketplace designed to connect Malawian influencers, creators, brands, businesses, churches, musicians, event organizers, and agencies in one platform. Influencers can create professional profiles, discover campaigns, apply for paid opportunities, receive payments, and showcase their social media reach.

On the other side, brands can create campaigns, find suitable influencers, review creator profiles, and manage marketing collaborations more easily. The goal is to make influencer marketing in Malawi more structured, accessible, measurable, and trusted.

Platform overview — connecting creators and brands

Product Type

Mobile app & Marketplace platform — serving two main user groups:

  • Influencers / Creators — users who want to get discovered, apply for campaigns, promote brands, and earn income through their audience and content.
  • Brands / Businesses — users who want to run campaigns, find influencers, promote products or services, and track collaboration opportunities.

The Problem

Influencer marketing is growing in Malawi, but the process is still mostly informal. Many brands want to work with creators, but they do not always know where to find reliable influencers. At the same time, many influencers have audiences and talent, but they do not have a structured way to get discovered, apply for campaigns, or prove their value to brands.

The current process often happens through WhatsApp, direct messages, referrals, or personal connections — creating several compounding problems.

The informal influencer marketing landscape in Malawi

  • Influencers struggle to find paid opportunities.
  • Brands struggle to identify the right creators.
  • Campaign communication becomes scattered.
  • Payments are not always clear or confirmed.
  • Influencer profiles are not standardized.
  • Smaller creators are often overlooked due to lack of visibility.
The main challenge was to create a platform where both sides can connect in a more organized, professional, and trustworthy way.

Key Challenges

Challenge 1 — Dual audience, different goals

Designing an app that feels professional enough for brands, but still simple and friendly for everyday creators. Some users may not be highly technical, so the experience needed to feel familiar, direct, and easy to complete.

Challenge 2 — Two-sided platform

Influencers and brands have different goals, but they still need to interact within the same ecosystem. The design needed to support both without making the app feel complicated or fragmented.

Challenge 3 — Trust

Brands need to trust that creator profiles are real and useful. Influencers also need to trust that campaigns and payments are legitimate. Profile completion, social media links, wallet screens, and transaction confirmations became critical design areas.

Visual Design Direction

The visual design uses a modern mobile-first style with a clean white background, rounded cards, red brand accents, and simple icons. The red primary color gives the app energy and confidence — it also helps important buttons stand out clearly.

Design choices:

  • Red primary color for buttons, highlights, active tabs, and important UI elements.
  • White and light grey backgrounds to keep the interface clean.
  • Card-based layouts for campaigns, creators, wallet items, and transactions.
  • Rounded input fields, profile cards, progress indicators, and category chips.
  • Bottom navigation with familiar mobile patterns.
  • Verified Pending Applied Completed — status labels for clarity.
The interface was designed to feel youthful, creator-friendly, and professional enough for business use — local without feeling limited.

Key UX Decisions

Decision 01
Multi-step Profile Completion
Instead of a single long form, profile setup is broken into smaller steps with a progress bar. This makes the process feel lighter — especially important since influencer profiles require personal details, social links, categories, and a photo.
Decision 02
Card-Based Layouts
Campaigns, influencers, wallet items, and transactions are displayed as cards. Cards make content more visual and easier to compare — essential for a marketplace where users need to scan multiple options quickly.
Decision 03
Strong Primary Action Buttons
Most critical actions — login, continue, deposit, withdraw, and apply — use red buttons. This creates consistency and helps users know what to do next without hesitation.
Decision 04
Social Media Platform Visibility
Because the app is about influencer marketing, social media platforms are shown clearly with recognizable icons for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and others — helping brands quickly understand where the influencer is active.
Decision 05
Wallet Transparency
Wallet screens show balance, transaction history, deposit flow, withdrawal flow, and confirmation states. Financial transparency builds trust — users need to know their earnings are being tracked correctly.
Decision 06
Local Market Fit
Designed for the Malawian market — supporting local creators, local brands, local campaign needs, and payment behavior that fits the environment. This gives the app a stronger identity than a generic influencer platform.

The final design creates a strong foundation for a local influencer marketing platform. The app gives creators a professional way to present themselves and gives brands a better way to discover and work with influencers.

The interface is simple, modern, and easy to understand. Onboarding, category selection, social media linking, wallet management, campaign discovery, and profile screens make the product feel complete and practical.

Influencer Malawi has the potential to become more than an app — it can become digital infrastructure for the creator economy in Malawi, helping creators turn visibility into opportunity and helping brands turn campaigns into measurable partnerships.
