FAQ

Frequent Asked
Questions

Answers to common questions about my work, processes, services and how I work

Contact me +
1. Are you available for freelance or full-time work? +

Yes — I'm actively seeking remote UI/UX roles with teams across the globe. I'm open to full-time positions, contract work, and freelance collaborations, and I'm comfortable working across time zones.

2. Where are you based and do you work remotely? +

I'm based in Malawi and fully set up for remote work. I collaborate with clients globally and adapt seamlessly to US and UK working hours.

3. How can I contact you? +

You can reach me via email or the contact form on this portfolio. I'm also available on LinkedIn and Behance.

4. What design tools and skills do you specialize in? +

I specialize in Figma for UI/UX design, prototyping, and design systems. My process covers user research, wireframing, interaction design, and handoff. I also have hands-on development experience in Flutter and React Native, which means I design with implementation in mind.

5. How do you charge (hourly or per project)? +

I typically charge per project depending on scope and complexity, but hourly rates can also be arranged for ongoing or part-time engagements.

6. What does your design process look like? +

I follow a user-centered design process: discovery and research, defining the problem, ideation and wireframing, high-fidelity UI design, interactive prototyping, and developer handoff. I keep stakeholders involved throughout to ensure the final product meets both user and business goals.
